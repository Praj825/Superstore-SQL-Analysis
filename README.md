# 🛒 Superstore Sales Analysis — SQL Project

## 📌 Project Overview
Analyzed **9,994 sales records** from a US-based Superstore using MySQL to uncover revenue patterns, profit drivers, discount impact, and regional performance.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **MySQL / MySQL Workbench**
- **Dataset:** Sample Superstore (Kaggle)

---

## 📊 Key Business Insights

| # | Insight |
|---|---|
| 1 | Total Revenue: **$2.29M** with **12.5% profit margin** |
| 2 | **West region** leads sales ($725K) but **Central** has dangerously low margin |
| 3 | **Furniture** category — only **2.49% profit margin** despite $742K sales |
| 4 | **Tables & Bookcases** are loss-making sub-categories |
| 5 | Discounts above **30% always result in loss** — tipping point identified |
| 6 | **Texas** — 3rd highest sales state but **-$25K loss** |
| 7 | **Home Office + Office Supplies** = highest margin combo at **20.84%** |
| 8 | **50% discount** orders average **-$310 loss per order** |

---

## 🔍 Queries Covered

1. Total Revenue, Profit & Orders — Business Overview
2. Region-wise Sales & Profit
3. Category-wise Sales, Profit & Margin %
4. Top 10 Loss-Making Sub-Categories
5. Discount vs Profit Impact Analysis
6. Customer Segment Analysis
7. Ship Mode Performance & Avg Order Value
8. Top 10 States by Sales
9. Profit Margin by Segment & Category
10. High Discount Orders in Loss

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- **Stop discounts above 30%** — every order above this threshold runs at a loss
- **Re-evaluate Furniture pricing** — especially Tables & Bookcases
- **Focus on Home Office segment** — highest profit margin at 20.84%
- **Investigate Texas & Ohio** — high sales but consistent losses
- **Promote Standard Class shipping** — handles 60% of orders efficiently
